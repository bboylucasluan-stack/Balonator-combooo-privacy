1 Information Collection

REI BALÃO - Arcade de Balões does not collect any personally identifiable information from users. The App operates entirely offline on your device.

We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or other contact information
  • Device identifiers such as IMEI, MAC address, or Advertising ID
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Photos, contacts, or other data from your device
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data

Game data such as high scores, settings, and progress may be saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the App.

2 Data Usage

Since REI BALÃO does not collect any personal data, we do not use, process, or share any information about you.

The App does not require an internet connection to function and does not communicate with any external servers. All game functionality is executed locally on your device.

No user accounts, logins, or authentication are required to play the game.

3 Third Party Services

REI BALÃO does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

Specifically, the App does not use:

  • Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or similar services
  • Advertising networks such as AdMob, Unity Ads, or Meta Audience Network
  • Social media SDKs like Facebook SDK
  • Crash reporting tools that transmit data
  • Payment processors or in-app purchase systems

Because no third-party services are used, no data is shared with external companies.

4 Children's Privacy

REI BALÃO is suitable for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar international regulations.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. Since the game is fully offline and requires no permissions, it is safe for children to use without parental supervision regarding privacy concerns.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please note that the App has no ability to receive or store such information. However, you may contact us if you have any concerns.
